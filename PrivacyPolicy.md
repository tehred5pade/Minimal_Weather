# Privacy Policy for Minimal Weather

**Effective Date:** November 2026

TehRed5pade built Minimal Weather as a free weather application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Access
* **Location Data:** With your permission, Minimal Weather accesses your device's approximate or precise location solely to retrieve local weather forecasts, and radar imagery for your area. 
* **Ephemeral Processing:** Your location coordinates are used strictly in real time to query weather and map services. We do not operate personal servers to store, log, or track your location history.

## 2. Third-Party Services
To display weather conditions and interactive maps, the app sends standard network requests (which include your requested coordinates and IP address) directly to third-party data providers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections:
* **Open-Meteo:** Used to retrieve hourly and daily weather forecast data.
* **LibreWXR:** Used to retrieve weather radar tile overlays.
* **OpenFreeMap / MapLibre:** Used to render interactive base map tiles.

These providers process requests in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Minimal Weather does not integrate advertising SDKs or sell user data to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage and Deletion
All app preferences (such as theme selection, saved locations, and temperature units) are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the app.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Minimal Weather is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
